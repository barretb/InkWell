# Daily word-count goals

Open **Daily goal** from a manuscript.

## Setting a target

Type a number of words per day. It saves as you type — there is no save button here either.

**Clear** stops tracking. Your history is kept, so clearing a goal is not the same as erasing what
you have already written.

## Today's progress

Progress shows as a sentence as well as a bar, because a bar alone is no use to anyone reading the
screen aloud or looking at it in bright sun:

> Today: 200 of 500 words · 300 to go · In progress

The states are:

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| **No goal** | You have not set a target. |
| **In progress** | You are below the target. |
| **Goal met** | You have written exactly the target. |
| **Goal exceeded** | You have written more than the target. |

*Met* and *exceeded* are distinguished on purpose, so hitting 500 exactly reads as an
accomplishment rather than as a near miss.

## What counts

Words written **today**, across every chapter of the manuscript, counted as prose only — Markdown
symbols and image markers are excluded, exactly as in the editor's counts.

Progress reflects the change in your chapters, so deleting a paragraph you wrote this morning takes
those words back off today's total. The number is what you actually added, not how many times you
pressed a key.

## Day rollover

The day turns over at midnight in **your device's local time**. If you are writing at 11:58pm and
carry on past midnight, the words after midnight count toward the new day, and the day you just
finished is stored in your history with the target that applied to it.

Your target is not reset by the rollover. Only the progress is.

## History

Prior days are listed newest first, each with what you wrote, the target that applied that day, and
whether you met it — in words, so it reads correctly aloud:

> 12 March 2026 — 640 of 500 words · Goal met
